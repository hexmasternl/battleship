Battleship design system

All stations report. Twelve by twelve grid, five hulls, one chance per turn.

This page renders every token and element of the Battleship design system. Dark only. Phones first. Built from the colours of the deep and the light of the bridge.

The written reference lives in docs/design-system.md. Everything here is plain HTML and CSS: no script, no build step. Copy the :root block from the source of this page to adopt the tokens.

9. Responsive notes

Tokens shown: --bs-bp-sm --bs-bp-md --bs-bp-lg --bs-bp-xl

This page is laid out for 360px first. Resize the window to see each breakpoint add layout: at 480px button rows stop stacking and swatch grids widen; at 768px the board sits beside its legend and instruments go two by two; at 1024px instruments sit in one row and scales show all ten steps across; at 1280px content is centred inside the 1200px container. Nothing scrolls horizontally at any width between 360px and 1920px; wide tables scroll inside their own box.
