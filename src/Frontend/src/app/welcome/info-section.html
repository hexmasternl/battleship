How the battle is fought

Two captains, two hidden fleets, one grid each. The first to sink every enemy ship wins.

How a game works

One captain creates a game and shares its code; the other joins with it. Both place their fleets in secret, then take turns calling a coordinate on the enemy grid until one fleet is at the bottom of the sea.

The fleet

Five ships, each one cell wide, placed in a straight line without overlapping.

The grid

Each fleet sails on a 12 by 12 grid: columns A to L across the top, rows 1 to 12 down the side. A shot is called by its column and row, such as D7, and every cell can be fired on once.

Turns and hits

Captains alternate. Every shot is answered with one of three results.

  • Miss. Open water; the turn passes.
  • Hit. Part of a ship took the shot.
  • Sunk. Every cell of that ship has been hit.
